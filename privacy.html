Privacy

Last updated 21 August 2026

AgentIsland runs entirely on your Mac. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that sees your work.

What never leaves your machine

Your prompts, the commands your agents run, tool inputs, file contents and diffs, repository paths, session transcripts, and the names of your projects. None of these are transmitted anywhere, under any setting, ever. The app reads them locally to draw its own interface and for nothing else.

What is sent, and only if you ask for it

  • Diagnostics — off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, the app may send its own version, your macOS version, and counts of successful and failed launches. The exact list is printed next to the switch.
  • Sponsor impressions — Pro only, and only while you have the sponsor slot switched on. Sponsored lines are never shown on the free tier, so a free installation reports nothing here at all. If a sponsored line is shown to you, the app reports how many times that happened, along with the app version and your licence account, which is the account the credit is applied to. No session data, no project names, no timings.
  • Licence checks. If you activate a licence key, that key and a per-machine activation identifier are sent to our payment provider to verify it, and re-checked periodically. That is the whole exchange.
  • Update checks. The app asks a static file whether a newer version exists. Standard web-server logs at that host may record your IP address, as they would for any download.

What is stored on your Mac

Usage history (per-session cost, token counts, model and project name) is kept locally for 90 days so the app can show you what your agents cost. It is never uploaded. You can clear it at any time, and removing the app removes it.

Files the app writes

Hook configuration in the CLIs you connect, and its own scripts, logs and settings in ~/Library/Application Support/AgentIsland. Every configuration file is named in the interface before you agree to it, backed up before the first edit, and restored when you disconnect.

Contact

Questions or a deletion request: write to support@tikistudios.io, or open an issue at the issue tracker if it does not need to be private.
