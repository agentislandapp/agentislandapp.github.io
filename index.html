What it does

Answer permission prompts without switching apps

The card shows the tool, the command, and a red/green diff for edits. Allow or deny with a click, or ⌥↩ and ⌥N from any app.

See every session at a glance

One dot per running session, tinted by which CLI it is, with a context-usage ring and a todo count. Nothing else, until something needs you.

Reply from anywhere

⌥Space opens a composer aimed at whichever session most needs you. Type or dictate; it is delivered through tmux, or typed into the terminal for you.

Know what it costs

Live model, context percentage and spend per session — and a history of what each project actually cost you this week.

Works with your CLI

Depth differs per tool, so it is stated per tool. The app says the same thing in its own Settings rather than implying more than it does.

Buy Pro

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record and charges in USD. You get a licence key by email; the same download becomes Pro once you paste the key into Settings → Licence. One licence covers three Macs and can be moved between them. If the app doesn't do what this page says it does, ask for a refund within 14 days and you'll get one.

The sponsor slot, later. There is no advertising in AgentIsland today and nothing has been shown to anyone. Once enough people are using it to be worth a sponsor's time, one sponsored line will appear beneath your own sessions — never over a permission request, never while an agent is working, at most once every four hours. Part of what the sponsor pays comes back to you as credit against your own subscription. It is off-switchable in Settings today, before it ever begins.

On Pro it is off, and stays off unless you turn it on. Buy Pro and change nothing and you will never be shown a sponsor. The switch exists there at all because the credit comes off a subscription — and Pro is where the subscription is. Offering the slot only to the free tier would have meant promising money to the one group with no bill to reduce.

Your machine stays yours

Everything runs locally. There is no account to create, and no telemetry unless you switch it on — in which case Settings prints the exact list of what would be sent, next to the switch. Prompts, commands, tool inputs, repository paths and transcripts are never sent anywhere, under any setting.

Every file the app writes to your home directory is named in the interface before you agree to it, backed up once before the first edit, and restored when you remove the integration — including your own statusLine.
